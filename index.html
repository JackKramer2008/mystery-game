Entrance

You finally stop second guessing yourself coming here and keep your legs moving.
"Hi." your friend Robin says, standing nearby the entrance while continuously flicking a lighter on and off like a lightswitch.
You stop. You haven't heard that voice in a long time. You grew apart for reasons that you still don't quite understand.
"Hey," You think about fist bumping or dapping to do something with your hands, but you don't. "Long time no see."
The tunnel entrance is smaller than you remember, but nevertheless familiar. Plants sprout all around, trying to cover up the industrial scar.
The water flowing through the tunnel mixes with the dirt and sunlight at the entrance to create the only green life that exists here.
"Shall we?" Robin asks without waiting for an ansewer. "Ladies first."
You nod a smile and step towards the opening.

The tunnel goes uphill steeply to your left and downhill slightly to your right.
It's dry for barely a step before a thin centimeter of water flows fast over your shoes. There's no going back from here.
Just to the left before it gets too steep, you see a swing far from the slippery stone floor.
Ropes have been tied to a metal grate above that then attach to a soft, bendy piece of wood that is meant for sitting.
Every inch of your sight has been tatooed by grafitti, some of it done by people you know.
Even the swing matches the walls.
