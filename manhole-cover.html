Manhole Cover

The sewers become plain again. The magic seems to fade, but you still feel it tugging you back. Each step seems harder than the last.
But maybe you're just tired. You've been walking for a while.
"Oh. We're here." Robin says.
You see a ladder on the wall that leads up to a manhole cover. A padlock binds the metal circle into place, unmoveable.
"It's been quite the trip, Robin." you speak. "I'll never forget it."
"Yeah... Don't." she points.
You gaze at the manhole cover, knowing what it means.
