The Surface

"We made it..." Robin says, absently.
"Now what?" You ask to the sky.
"I should be going, I'm expected." she sighs.
"It was nice catching up. Good to see you, Robin."
"Yeah definently." she smiles. "Bye."
She goes back to her life and you go back to yours. It's quiet and you think to yourself about the whole thing the entire walk home. You have things to do. Responsibilities. You feel accomplished, but heavy.
Who knows if you'll ever do this or see her again. Life has a way of doing that.
But it was kinda nice while it lasted.
