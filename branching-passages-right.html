Branching Passages

You head to the right. The tunnel narrows until you and Robin eventually have to crouch down. Robin takes out her phone flashlight and shines it on the colorful art-filled walls. The tunnel soon comes to a dead end where a mural is painted on the wall of two people kissing. Suddenly, Robin turns around toward you, muttering, "Dead end."
Before you can stop your feet, you crash into her and she crashes into you. Robin gets up quickly, holding herself off the ground with her arms directly over you.
"Sorry!" you both say at the same time. A moment goes by as she stares down at you and you stare up at her, stare at each until she pushes herself back into crouching position and helps you up.
"Let's get going now. There's more to see," Robin says.
