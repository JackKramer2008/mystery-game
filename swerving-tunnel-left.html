Swerving Tunnels

You walk on the left side of the tunnels, across from Robin. She's slightly slower than you, so you take the lead. Suddenly, you hear a pipe flushing water into the sewers right in front of you. There's nowhere dry to go, it's too far to leap across to the right side. Your feet become soaked and you can't help but let out a dicontented moan as Robin stifles a laugh. You've minorly embarrassed yourself.
