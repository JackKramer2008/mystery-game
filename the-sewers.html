The Sewers

You and Robin look at each other for a long moment and recognition fills the air between you.
The sewers seem to close in around the two of you until you are standing very close.
You've escaped, in a way. Not from the sewers, but from the surface. From all those problems and things to do up there.
And you're together.
"Are you okay with this?" You ask.
"I didn't think I would be, but I am. As long as you are too, 100%."
"I am."
"Okay."
"Okay."
"Just," she pauses. "Don't restart. Whatever you do don't restart. Because then what's the point of forever. We can go anywhere."
"Do you think we'll lose our minds if we're here forever?" You question, just in case.
"I don't think anyone ever really has their minds, you know? Let's just pretend for a while. Do all the things we've done. All the good times down here."

Where do you go?
