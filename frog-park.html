Frog Park

You don't know how much time passes as you trudge forward in silence through the deep water. Some sections are deeper than others, but by the end of it, even the bottom of your shirt is soaked.
You finally see light again up ahead, and the water gets shallower and shallower until it is just barely past your ankles.
You come to a very well lit chamber. Sun enters through a grate that takes up the whole ceiling. A ladder leads up to the top, but there is no visible exit or entrance. The graffiti on the walls is picturesque and Robin stops and stares.
"This is frog park. This is below frog park... I used to come here all the time as a kid."
"Down here?" you ask.
"No up above. There's like a playground and everything. I used to stand up there on top of the grate and drop sticks through the holes."
As she continues talking, you pull your phone out of your pocket.
