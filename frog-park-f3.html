Frog Park

You walk towards Robin and ask.
"Flick time?"
"Sure."
You stand beside her and flip the camera around.
She puts her arm around your shoulder.
You lean into each other to get in frame, and you take the picture.
And one more picture.
"You're sending me that." Robin demands as she pulls away. "I'm printing it out and putting it on the wall."
Her smile is infectious.
She turns towards the tunnel that continues forward.
