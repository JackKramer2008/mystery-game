Chandelier Room

The walk is short and shallow. Following Robin seems to make time speed up, but suddenly Robin stops.
"Whoa."
You see a chandelier hanging from the ceiling. More chandeliers line the ceiling further up.
Little alcoves somehow have been carved into the walls where candlesticks make their home.
"It's like... medieval europe. Or something." Robin remarks.
"Except for the anime graffiti." you joke.
You can't see her smile, but you know she does.
Someone has placed planks on the shoulder of the sewer to form a sort of bench. You feel it with your hand, and it's very sturdy.
"It's so pretty." Robin's voice echoes.
The water seems to run slower here, so everything is quieter.
This place feels magical and ancient. Is this a home? Or a crypt?
