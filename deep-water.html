Deep Water

You walk for a long while until the water gradually begins to get deeper. And deeper.
And deeper.
Everything is very still, as if the whole world is scared to breath. A single ray of light illuminates the glassy surface of the water. Robin whispers, "You should roll up your pants for this part." She pulls up her leggings as she continues forward.
However, a glint of something shiny catches your eye, at the bottom of the waist deep water up ahead.
