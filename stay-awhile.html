Nowhere And Everywhere

You sit down on the makeshift bench, and Robin slowly moves towards you like a magnet.
The two of you watch the chandeliers, the candlesticks, the water, the colorful graffiti.
Your bodies are close and almost touching. In the dark you, you try to imagine what the two of you look like right now.
Your imagination seems to become reality. Time is a forgotten memory as the two of you exist together in this perfect space.
After what feels like an eternity and only a moment all at once, you finally break apart, standing up off the bench.
You stretch the dust off your bones and look at each other in the darkness.
"What now?" Robin ends the silence.
