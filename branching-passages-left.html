Branching Passages

You head to the left. You can stand up in this tunnel, but it seems to be in incredible desrepair. Rough exposed rock contrasts the smoothness of the tunnel from whence you came. The smell is putrid and old, and it begins to get stronger the further down you go. As you continue, you see the darkness come to an end. A dead end. With a dead body. The source of the smell is evident. The slumped decomposing form of someone who long ago was a person is propped against the dead end wall. "Oh my god..." Robin says breathlessly. "That's so disgusting... and sad."
"What happend?" you wonder aloud.
"Can we leave?" Robin turns around. "Like... now?"
You follow her out, and ask if she's okay.
"Yeah, I'm... fine. Just don't like dead bodies."
She seems to avoid your eyes, but you can't quite tell in the darkness.
